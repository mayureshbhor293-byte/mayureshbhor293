Porsche Car Features

Performance-focused engines
Turbocharged petrol engines and advanced electric drivetrains.
Acceleration
Many models achieve 0–100 km/h in under 4 seconds (911 Turbo S: 2.5s, Taycan Turbo: 2.7s).
Top speeds
Range from 232 km/h (Macan) to 322 km/h (911 Turbo S).
Drive options
Rear-wheel drive (RWD) and all-wheel drive (AWD).
Luxury interiors
Premium leather, customizable trims, advanced infotainment.
Technology
Porsche Communication Management (PCM), adaptive aerodynamics, launch control, and driver-assist features.
Electric innovation
Taycan and Macan Electric models with high-performance battery systems.
